# Software-Development-Lifecycle---Sprint-Review-and-Retrospective-

This document reflects on the development process of the SNHU Travel application pilot, specifically focusing on our transition to the Scrum-Agile framework. The shift was essential for improving team accountability, quality assurance, and, crucially, our ability to adapt quickly to non-negotiable changes, such as the mid-sprint requirement for a slideshow-based destination list.

How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?

Interpreting a user's needs involves shifting focus from the technical features; instead, we need to focus on the overall goal or underlying problem. This is best done through the use of user stories, which are concise statements of what the end user wants. A good example is that I, the user, like the goal for its benefits. This helps establish some acceptable criteria for a checklist for future teams and an acceptable 'checklist' of what is desired by the end users.

How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I want to incorporate into my future development work?

The agile methodology is fantastic for developing and delivering working software frequently while making it adaptable to change. In the future, some aspects I plan to include in future development work are a Spirit review to assess my performance on a software development project and ensure I have addressed all necessary counterpoints. This is in preparation for a sprint planning session to help establish what the end user hopes to accomplish during development.

What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

Being an effective team member is not just about being able to write and design functioning code. It is the ability to establish collaboration with my teammates, understanding my specific roles in the development cycle while being upfront and transparent with any issues I am facing. Everyone struggles sometimes; we shouldn't be afraid to share these challenges.
